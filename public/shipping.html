Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

Fulfillment Overview: All orders are carefully hand-inspected and packed from our central botanical apothecary facility in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India.

1. Nationwide Delivery Coverage

UMANARAYNI ships to all valid 6-digit postal PIN codes across all 28 Indian States and 8 Union Territories. During checkout, our real-time PIN code verification engine validates serviceability before order payment.

2. Order Processing & Dispatch Timeline

  • Standard Orders: Dispatched within 24 to 48 business hours following payment confirmation (excluding Sundays and national holidays).
  • UPI QR / Manual Orders: Dispatched within 24 to 48 business hours after our accounts team completes bank statement UTR reconciliation.

3. Estimated Delivery Times

Once dispatched from our fulfillment facility, estimated transit times are as follows:

  • Metro Cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Chennai): 2 to 4 business days.
  • Tier 2 & Tier 3 Cities: 3 to 6 business days.
  • Rest of India & Remote / North-East Regions: 5 to 8 business days.

Note: Extreme weather, regional logistical disruptions, or festival peaks may occasionally cause minor transit delays.

4. Shipping Charges & Free Shipping Threshold

  • Orders ₹499 and Above: FREE Standard Delivery nationwide.
  • Orders Below ₹499: A nominal flat shipping and handling fee of ₹49 is applied at checkout.

5. Courier Partners & Tracking

Shipments are routed through premier national courier and logistics partners. Upon dispatch, an automated shipment tracking link is sent directly to your registered mobile number and email.

6. Damaged or Tampered Shipments

If your package appears physically damaged, opened, or tampered with at the time of delivery, please refuse delivery and notify our WhatsApp helpline (+91 8641033514) within 24 hours with photos of the outer package.
