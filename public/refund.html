Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

7-Day Easy Return Commitment: We stand behind the botanical purity and craftsmanship of our classical herbal personal care formulations. If you receive a damaged, leaked, or incorrect formulation, we offer a hassle-free 7-day replacement or refund.

1. Return Eligibility & Hygiene Standards

Due to stringent hygiene, safety, and regulatory standards governing personal care and cosmetic preparations (oils, shampoos, ubtan powders, and face washes), return requests are subject to the following conditions:

  • Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Items: If your product arrives broken, leaking, or differs from the formulation ordered, report it within 7 days of delivery for an immediate free replacement or full refund.
  • Unopened & Unused Goods: General returns must be completely unopened, in their original botanical packaging with safety seals unbroken.
  • Non-Returnable: Opened jars, partially used formulations, or items without original outer packaging cannot be returned for hygiene and health safety reasons.

2. Order Cancellation Policy

  • Before Dispatch: You may cancel your order at any time before it leaves our botanical fulfillment hub. We will issue a 100% refund immediately.
  • After Dispatch: Once handed over to our logistics partner and in transit, orders cannot be cancelled mid-route. You may refuse delivery upon arrival, and the refund will be processed upon package return.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

Once your return is received and inspected at our returns facility:

  • Prepaid Orders (PhonePe / Cards / Net Banking): Refund is credited directly to the original payment source within 5 to 7 business days.
  • UPI QR Payments: Refund is transferred directly to your bank account via UPI within 24 to 48 hours of verification.
